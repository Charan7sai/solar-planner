Monthly generation compared with your use

Bars show how many units the recommended system would generate each month in a typical year (the middle value of 20 years of weather). The dashed line is your monthly use. Where a bar rises above the line, the surplus is exported to the grid and earns the export rate. Generation dips in the monsoon months because of cloud cover.

    Cumulative savings over 25 years

    The line starts below zero at your net cost after subsidy and rises with each year of bill savings. The year it crosses zero is your payback point; everything after that is profit. Savings grow faster later because tariffs are assumed to rise 3% a year, while panels lose 0.5% of output a year.

      How generation varies from year to year

      Each bar is what the same system would have generated with the actual weather of that year from 2004 to 2023. The solid line is the typical year (P50) and the dashed line is the poor year figure (P90), the output exceeded in 9 out of 10 years. Lenders and installers use P90 as the safe planning number. The scale does not start at zero, so the differences between years look larger than they are.

        Subsidy follows central financial assistance for residential systems under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. Extra subsidies from states are not included. Actual output depends on shade, panel tilt and installation quality.
